A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Before you start.

Is Meu free?

The Meu application is open source under the MIT license and free to download. Your model provider and optional external services may have their own charges. Local model requirements depend on the model and your hardware.

Does Meu only work with DeepSeek?

No. Meu supports multiple providers, including Anthropic, DeepSeek, Gemini, OpenAI Codex and OpenRouter, plus compatible local endpoints. Authentication methods and available models depend on the provider.

How do updates work?

Open Settings → About → Version & updates to check for an update and start installation. Preview installers and release notes are published on GitHub.

Is this related to Hermes Agent?

Meu is based on Hermes Agent and is maintained as an independent project, with its own Windows distribution, privacy changes and coordinator work. Upstream authorship and MIT copyright notices are preserved.

Where can I ask questions or report a problem?

Use GitHub Discussions for ideas and questions, or open an issue for a bug. English and Greek feedback are welcome. Remove keys, tokens and private information from any logs or screenshots you share.
